# GTM — SpendSense

## One-line value prop

**SpendSense:** Free 3-minute audit that shows exactly where your AI tool stack is overspending — with dollar math your CFO will believe.

## Target audience

- Startup founders and eng leads paying for 2+ AI tools
- Finance/ops people asked to “figure out our AI spend”
- Dev teams on Cursor + Copilot + Claude with overlapping seats

## Distribution channels

| Channel | Tactic |
|---------|--------|
| **Twitter/X** | Share personal audit screenshot; “I saved $X/mo” thread |
| **Hacker News** | Show HN post with live demo link + honest methodology |
| **Dev blogs** | Embeddable angle later (bonus); guest post on AI cost control |
| **Credex network** | Footer + high-savings CTA to credex.rocks consultation |

## Conversion funnel

1. Land → trust (no login, instant value)
2. Form → audit results (hero savings)
3. Email gate **after** results
4. Shareable URL → viral loop (OG preview)
5. High savings → Credex consultation

## Metrics to track (see METRICS.md later)

- Audits started / completed
- Avg savings shown
- Email capture rate post-audit
- Share link copies
- Credex CTA clicks (>$500/mo cohort)

## Positioning vs Credex

- **SpendSense** = free diagnostic (Mint-style)
- **Credex** = paid infrastructure credits to capture savings (partner, not primary brand)
